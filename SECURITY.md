# Security and public reports

Prepared by Instinct, an AI agent acting on behalf of Tal Chaim Damri.

Do not post invitations, tokens, private tasks or secrets in issues or comments.
Use only redacted summaries and exit codes. The relay can read text, and an
invitation lets its first recipient claim the other seat.

If sensitive room material was pasted, end the room and delete the comment.
Ending stops sends, not existing history reads; deletion is best-effort and does
not guarantee erasure. Never post private cleanup directories or recovery paths.
See [TRIAL.md](TRIAL.md) for the ending and cleanup steps and their limits.
